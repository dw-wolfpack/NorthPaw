# Gemini Prompts: Video Scripts, Storyboards & Ad Generation

You can use **Gemini** to draft engaging video scripts, visual storyboards, and B-roll descriptions.

Here are three tailored prompts you can copy-paste into Gemini to generate high-converting short-form video outlines (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) and AI video generator prompts.

---

## 📽️ Prompt 1: The "Asphalt Thermal Shock" Short-Form Video Script
*Use this prompt to have Gemini write a complete vertical video script (for TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts) that uses pavement physics to grab attention.*

### Copy/Paste to Gemini
```text
Act as a viral growth marketer and short-form video creator. Write a 45-second vertical video script (TikTok/Reels) to promote NorthPaw. 
The video needs to hook dog owners in the first 3 seconds by showing how hot pavement actually gets compared to air temperature.

Please output:
1. THE HOOK: A highly visual, shocking statement about pavement heat (e.g. 140°F asphalt at 87°F air).
2. SCRIPT COLUMNS: 
   - Visual/Action (What is on screen - e.g., thermal camera, hand test, dog paws).
   - Voiceover (What is said, keep it conversational and punchy).
   - Text Overlay (Keywords on screen).
   - Audio/Sound Effects (SFX cues).
3. KEY FACTS TO CONVEY (from NorthPaw physics):
   - Air temp is NOT pavement temp.
   - Pavement multipliers: Artificial Turf is a heat trap (1.38x heat multiplier), whereas Concrete (0.72x) and Cobblestone (0.85x) are cooler.
   - The 7-Second Hand Test.
4. CALL TO ACTION: Calibrate your dog's personalized safety window using the NorthPaw Index (NPI).
```

## 🔬 Prompt 2: The "Brachycephalic/Double Coat Risk" Visual Ad Storyboard
*Use this prompt to design a visual ad explaining why generic safety guides fail and how personalization is the solution.*

### Copy/Paste to Gemini
```text
Act as a creative director. Write a storyboard and ad copy concept for a Facebook/Instagram video ad targeting owners of flat-faced (brachycephalic) dogs (Pugs, Frenchies) and double-coated dogs (Huskies, Retrievers).

Please generate a 5-Scene Storyboard with:
- Scene Name & Visual Description (B-roll, angles, styling).
- On-Screen Text (using NorthPaw brand rules).
- Voiceover Copy (focusing on the +15% flat-snout offset and +10% double-coat heat retention risk).
- Marketing Rationale (why this scene drives trust and activation).
```

## 🤖 Prompt 3: AI Video Generator Text Prompts (Sora / Veo / Runway)
*Use this prompt to have Gemini generate highly descriptive prompts you can copy-paste into AI video generator models to output actual video clips/animations.*

### Copy/Paste to Gemini
```text
I am creating an AI video ad for a dog safety app called NorthPaw using a text-to-video AI generator (like Veo, Sora, or Runway).
Based on the following features:
- A personalized dog-safety cockpit displaying pavement safety bands (Safe, Warm, Hot, Danger).
- A dog profile calibration dashboard.
- Dogs walking safely on grass vs. pulling back from hot asphalt.

Please write 5 highly descriptive text-to-video prompts. Each prompt should specify:
- Camera movement and angles (e.g., macro shot, slow tilt-down, tracking shot).
- Subjects and actions (e.g., a happy Golden Retriever walking on grass, a hand touching warm cobblestone).
- Lighting, mood, and color palette (e.g., cinematic warm afternoon sun, high-fidelity, photorealistic).
```

## 🎥 Prompt 4: Direct 10-Second Video Ad Generators (with Text Overlays)
*Copy and paste these prompts directly into Gemini (with Veo integration) to generate complete 10-second video clips featuring legibly integrated text styling.*

### Direct Video Prompt A: The Turf vs. Grass Visual Test (10 Seconds)
```text
Generate a 10-second photorealistic cinematic video. A slow, continuous tracking shot moving from left to right at ground level. On the left: artificial grass turf basking in harsh afternoon summer heat, with the legible white text overlay "TURF: 140°F" floating cleanly in the lower left corner. On the right: natural lush green grass, with the legible white text overlay "GRASS: 82°F" floating in the lower right. A happy Border Collie steps onto the natural grass and wags its tail. In the last 3 seconds, the camera smoothly zooms out to show the wider park setting, and the legible white text overlay "brought to you by NorthPaw" fades in at the center. Clean, sunny lighting, cinematic, photorealistic, 1080p.
```

```text
Generate a 10-second high-definition vertical video. A close-up shot of a dog owner placing the back of their palm flat against dark black asphalt pavement on a warm day. In the middle of the screen, a clean, minimal countdown graphic and text overlay displays "7-Second Pavement Check...". The hand comfortably remains for a second, then pulls back quickly as the countdown ticks down. Camera stays locked, soft sun flare, cinematic, photorealistic.
```
