# NotebookLM Studio Creation Guide

Beyond the interactive chat, the **NotebookLM Studio** allows you to generate persistent, structured guide documents. These tools are perfect for assembling shareable marketing assets, press kits, clinical safety sheets, and launch playbooks.

Use the custom prompts below to generate these specific Studio artifacts.

---

## 📄 1. Briefing Document: "The Canine Safety Value Proposition Canvas"
*Use the Studio's **Briefing Document** tool to create a core marketing and positioning summary that you can share with copywriters, designers, or potential partners.*

### Generation Prompt
```text
Generate a Briefing Document outlining the NorthPaw Value Proposition.
Structure it into the following sections:
1. THE PROBLEM: Pavement heat-retention hazards (asphalt, turf, cobblestone vs air temperature) and systemic canine heat stress.
2. THE TARGET AUDIENCE PROFILE: Conscientious dog owners who enjoy walks, parks, and moderate outings.
3. THE SOLUTION: Real-time road temperature calculations and the personalized NorthPaw Index (NPI) score.
4. UNIQUE DIFFERENTIATORS: Personalized snout/coat calibration, science-anchored calculations, and local-first privacy.
5. MARKETING MESSAGING ANCHOR: The primary message ("Know what matters before you go") and how to express it in under 5 seconds.
```

## ❓ 2. FAQ Guide: "The Skeptical Dog Owner Objection Handler"
*Use the Studio's **FAQ** tool to compile a guide for handling user concerns. This is directly useful for writing landing page copy, app store descriptions, and customer support macros.*

### Generation Prompt
```text
Generate an FAQ Guide addressing potential user skepticism and technical questions.
Key questions to formulate and answer using the source data:
- "Why can't I just use a standard weather app to check if it is safe to walk my dog?"
- "How does snout shape (brachycephalic vs dolichocephalic) affect a dog's heat tolerance?"
- "Is cobblestone really safer than asphalt, and what about concrete?"
- "Why does the app ask for my dog's coat type and color during calibration?"
- "Is my location data sent to third-party ad networks or tracked continuously?"
- "What standard of safety does the 125°F pavement threshold represent?"
```

## 📖 3. Study Guide: "NorthPaw Science & Safety Calibration Cheat Sheet"
*Use the Studio's **Study Guide** tool to generate a resource. This is highly useful for onboarding influencers, creating content outlines for your blog, or distributing to vet clinics.*

### Generation Prompt
```text
Generate a Study Guide designed to educate partners and influencers on canine heat physics.
Include:
1. KEY CONCEPTS: Explain Canine Heat Stress Index (CHSI), Solar Load, Wind Convection, and Pavement Multipliers (especially artificial turf).
2. SHORT-ANSWER QUESTIONS & ANSWERS: Test comprehension on why brachycephalic dogs have a +15% risk multiplier and how high activity affects walk window durations.
3. CLINICAL ANCHORS: Detail the JAMA Dermatology burn standards (125°F threshold) and the American Kennel Club (AKC) guidelines used by the app.
```
