```text
Write three 30-second video script outlines for TikTok/Instagram Reels.
- Hook: Focus on the "7-Second Hand Test" (JAMA/AKC standards) and contrast it with NorthPaw's thermodynamic pavement calculator.
- Structure: Introduce the problem (pavement gets much hotter than air), show the math in action (e.g., concrete vs. turf), and show the app's timeline forecast.
- Style: Keep it fast-paced, highly visual, and educational. Suggest visual shots and overlays using the design rules in the sources.
```

### Prompt 2: Twitter/Threads Educational Threads
```text
Draft a 6-part educational thread for Twitter/Threads based on the NPI algorithm guide.
- Tweet 1: Start with a surprising hook about pavement temperature (e.g., "At 87°F air temp, asphalt can reach 140°F in under a minute. Here's the science why...").
- Tweet 2-4: Explain the variables (solar declination, humidity, wind, and pavement multipliers like concrete at 0.72 vs. artificial turf at 1.38).
- Tweet 3: Highlight the canine physiological component (snout profiles and coat styles).
- Tweet 6: Close with a call to action to try NorthPaw for personalized safety calibration.
```
