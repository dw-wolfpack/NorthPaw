# NotebookLM Infographic Generation Guide

NotebookLM's **Infographic** tool is a powerful way to generate visual, highly shareable social graphics (perfect for Instagram, Pinterest, or local flyer inserts).

Here are three distinct infographic concepts customized for **NorthPaw**, with their recommended styles, layouts, and generation prompts.

---

## 🛣️ Concept 1: The Pavement Heat Threat (Surface Multipliers)
*This compares how hot different pavement materials (asphalt, concrete, cobblestone, turf) get compared to the air temperature, highlighting the danger of paw burns.*

*   **Recommended Visual Style**: `Scientific` or `Bento Grid`
*   **Recommended Orientation**: `Portrait` (ideal for Pinterest / Instagram Stories) or `Landscape`
*   **Level of Detail**: `Standard` or `Detailed`
*   **Generation Prompt (Copy/Paste)**:
    ```text
    Create an infographic displaying pavement safety temperatures for dogs. Compare how different surfaces retain heat under solar load: Asphalt (baseline 1.0), Concrete (0.72 multiplier), Cobblestone (0.85 multiplier), Sand (1.15 multiplier), and Artificial Turf (1.38 heat trap multiplier). Highlight the JAMA Dermatology fact: "At 87°F air temp, asphalt can reach 140°F, causing burns in 60 seconds." Use an outdoor forest-green brand theme with high-contrast safety colors. Clearly separate the safety thresholds: Safe (<77°F), Warm (77-105°F), Hot (105-125°F), and Danger (>=125°F).
    ```

---

## 🐕 Concept 2: Canine Biology & The NPI Score
*This explains how a dog's personal biology (snout shape, coat type, activity) influences their systemic heat stress and calibration.*

*   **Recommended Visual Style**: `Instructional` or `Bento Grid`
*   **Recommended Orientation**: `Square` (ideal for Instagram posts) or `Portrait`
*   **Level of Detail**: `Standard`
*   **Generation Prompt (Copy/Paste)**:
    ```text
    Create an infographic showing how a dog's personal anatomy and behavior affects their outdoor safety. Outline the 3 key calibration inputs of the NorthPaw Index (NPI): 1) Snout Profile (flat-faced/brachycephalic dogs receive +15% risk because they cool less efficiently by panting), 2) Coat Type (double-coated dogs receive +10% heat retention risk), and 3) Activity Baseline (high-intensity dogs reduce safe walk windows by 20%). Use a modern, friendly, high-contrast color scheme. Keep text brief and let the layout guide the reader through the 3 biological calibration steps.
    ```

---

## 📝 Concept 3: The 3-Step Pre-Walk Safety Checklist
*A quick, practical checklist outlining the "7-Second Hand Test" and what to prepare before walking out the door.*

*   **Recommended Visual Style**: `Sketch Note` or `Instructional`
*   **Recommended Orientation**: `Portrait`
*   **Level of Detail**: `Concise` or `Standard`
*   **Generation Prompt (Copy/Paste)**:
    ```text
    Create a quick pre-walk safety checklist infographic for dog owners. Structure it into three simple steps: 1) Check the Conditions (local temperature & humidity), 2) Run the 7-Second Hand Test (place hand flat on pavement for 7 seconds; if it is too hot to hold, it is too hot for paws), 3) Pack the Essentials (water, towel, boot protection). Use a warm sketch-note style with clean borders. Accent with Safety Green and Warning Amber colors to make the check steps look actionable and friendly.
    ```
