# NorthPaw Boilerplate

## 50 Words

> NorthPaw is a privacy-first dog safety app that helps owners make better outdoor decisions using personalized weather, pavement temperature estimates, and canine heat-risk guidance. Everything runs locally on the device—no accounts, no ads, and no unnecessary data collection.

---

## 100 Words

NorthPaw helps dog owners make better outdoor decisions before every walk, hike, or adventure.

Rather than acting like another weather app, NorthPaw interprets current conditions specifically for your dog using breed characteristics, coat type, size, snout profile, weather, and pavement conditions.

The app is privacy-first, runs locally on the device, and requires no account. It combines transparent environmental models with practical guidance to answer one question in seconds:

**"Is it safe for my dog right now?"**

---

## Long Boilerplate

NorthPaw is a privacy-first mobile app that helps dog owners make more confident outdoor decisions.

By combining weather, pavement temperature estimates, and each dog's unique biological characteristics, NorthPaw provides personalized readiness guidance before walks, hikes, road trips, and outdoor adventures.

Unlike traditional weather apps, NorthPaw interprets environmental conditions specifically for dogs while remaining transparent about uncertainty and educational in its recommendations.

The app stores personal information locally on the device, requires no account, and is built around the belief that better information leads to safer adventures and healthier dogs.

# Founder Bio

## Short

Christopher Fiegel is an independent machine learning engineer and lifelong dog lover who built NorthPaw after realizing most people had no reliable way to understand how outdoor conditions affect their dogs.

Together with his Mini Australian Shepherd, Aoife, he created NorthPaw to help owners make safer outdoor decisions through privacy-first technology.

---

## Long

Christopher Fiegel is an independent software engineer specializing in machine learning and AI systems.

Outside of work, he spends much of his time running, hiking, and exploring with his Mini Australian Shepherd, Aoife.

After repeatedly seeing dangerous misconceptions around pavement temperatures and canine heat stress, he built NorthPaw to provide dog owners with fast, trustworthy guidance before heading outside.

NorthPaw is independently developed and continuously improved through direct conversations with dog owners around the world.
