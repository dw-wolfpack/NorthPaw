🎧 Audio Overview (Podcast)
These are for hearing your company from another perspective.
1. The Founder Story ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Tell the story of NorthPaw from the very beginning until today. Explain why it exists, how it evolved, what the biggest turning points were, what users changed, what nearly went wrong, and where the company is heading.

2. The Product Story ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Explain NorthPaw as if discussing one of the best designed utility apps ever built. Focus on product philosophy, user experience, trust, roadmap evolution, and long-term vision.

3. The Founder Operating System ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Describe how the founder thinks. What principles consistently appear? What tradeoffs does the founder make? What should never change?

4. Five Years From Now
Imagine NorthPaw in 2031 if execution goes extremely well.

5. Postmortem
Imagine NorthPaw failed in three years. Explain exactly why.
