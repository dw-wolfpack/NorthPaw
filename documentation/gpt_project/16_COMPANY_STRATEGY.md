# 16 — Company Strategy

> NorthPaw is not a weather app.
> NorthPaw is building the world's most trusted outdoor decision engine for dogs.

Last updated: 2026-08-02

---

# The Core Idea

NorthPaw is one decision engine.

Everything else exists to improve one output:

> **Help dog owners make a better outdoor decision.**

The inputs will evolve forever.

The output should always remain simple, trustworthy, and understandable.

# The Four Company Risks

NorthPaw succeeds by reducing four major uncertainties.

Nothing else matters until these are solved.

---

## Risk #1 — Acquisition

**Question**

Can enough dog owners discover NorthPaw?

**Success looks like**

- Growing weekly active users
- Organic sharing
- Veterinary distribution
- Shelter partnerships
- Press coverage
- ASO growth
- Word-of-mouth

**Primary owner**

Marketing & Growth

**Current belief**

Early evidence is encouraging.

The product appears significantly stronger than current distribution.

---

## Risk #2 — Retention

**Question**

Can NorthPaw become a year-round habit?

The engine must remain useful across every season.

Summer alone is not enough.

**Approach**

Expand the decision engine—not the interface.

Examples:

- Heat
- AQI
- Smoke
- Winter
- Ice
- Salt
- Pollen
- Tick exposure
- Future environmental risks

Every new capability should feed the same decision engine.

**Primary owner**

Product & Engineering

---

## Risk #3 — Monetization

**Question**

Can Companion provide enough additional value that users happily pay?

Core safety remains free forever.

Companion sells insight—not access.

Examples:

- Walk history
- Seasonal trends
- Personalized tolerance
- Favorite conditions
- Historical comparisons
- Personalized recommendations
- Companion intelligence

Engineering challenges include:

- Efficient local storage
- On-device personalization
- Lightweight local models
- Portable user-owned data
- Privacy-first architecture

**Primary owner**

Engineering

---

## Risk #4 — Business

If Risks 1–3 are solved, NorthPaw becomes a business.

The order matters.

Do not optimize monetization before proving acquisition.

Do not optimize acquisition before proving retention.

Do not build features that don't reduce one of the first three risks.

---

# Strategic Filter

Every significant feature should answer one question:

Which company risk does this reduce?

If the answer is "none,"

don't build it.

---

# Guiding Principle

NorthPaw is not trying to become another weather app.

NorthPaw is building the world's most trusted outdoor decision engine for dogs.

Everything else feeds the engine.
