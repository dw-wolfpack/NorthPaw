# 5.3 Retrospective

**Release:** 5.3.0 — Polish, Accessibility, Stability
**Shipped:** August 1, 2026 (submitted to App Store Connect)

---

## Goal

Ship a polished quality release.

## What went well

- Accessibility feedback was excellent
- Timer bug fixed
- Review flow turned out nicely
- Onboarding is much faster
- Share cards came together

## Biggest surprises

The biggest issues weren't algorithms.

They were:

- readability
- contrast
- spacing

## Bugs that escaped

- Timer lifecycle
- Current temp fallback
- Timeline overwrite
- Checklist toggle

*(All four fixed in 5.3 — timer reset + AppState safeguards, hourly temp fallback with 5-case regression suite, timeline pill/box alignment, checklist toggling.)*

## User feedback that changed the product

### Gabriel — Accessibility

- Photo backgrounds
- Contrast
- Spacing

→ Drove the sweeping accessibility contrast pass and the move to solid backgrounds on utility screens.

### Karel

- Timer freeze (fixed)

### Future requests (parked)

- AQI → 6.0
- Multiple dogs → 6.0

## What I'd do differently

- Earlier QA
- Real-device testing sooner
- More screenshots

## What NOT to do again

**Never use text over photos without validating contrast.**

## Biggest lesson

Users cared far more about usability than another feature.
