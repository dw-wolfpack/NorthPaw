NorthPaw Weekly Growth Flow

Mission: Build a product people trust, improve it through community feedback, and consistently create new ways for people to discover it.

⸻

Weekly North Star

Every week ask yourself:

Did I make NorthPaw better?

Did more people discover it?

Everything else is secondary.

⸻

Success Metrics

Product

* New feature requests
* Bugs fixed
* App Store reviews
* User emails
* Shares
* Weekly Safety Checks (WAU)

Growth

* New users
* Returning users
* Organic installs
* Social engagement
* Outreach conversations

⸻

Monday — Review & Plan 📊

Review Analytics

* Open Mixpanel dashboard
* Check New Users
* Check WAU
* Review Activation Funnel
* Review Weekly Safety Checks
* Review Shares
* Review Feedback Submitted

Review Community

* Read every App Store review
* Read every email
* Read every feedback request
* Update backlog.md

Plan

Choose ONE growth experiment.

Examples:

* Reddit
* Instagram
* TikTok
* Facebook Groups
* YouTube Shorts
* Local outreach
* Vet clinics
* Trail groups

⸻

Tuesday — Distribution 🚀

Create opportunities for people to discover NorthPaw.

Examples:

* 3 helpful Reddit comments
* 1 Reddit post
* 1 Facebook Group post
* 1 outreach email
* 1 local community outreach
* Contact one publication
* Reach out to one veterinarian
* Reach out to one rescue

Rule:

Never spam.

Always provide value first.

⸻

Wednesday — Content 🎥

Produce one piece of content.

Repurpose it everywhere.

Platforms:

* Instagram Reel
* TikTok
* YouTube Short
* Threads
* X

Content ideas:

* Feature spotlight
* Dog safety tips
* Breed spotlight
* Weather facts
* Building in public
* User feedback stories
* Development progress
* Before/After updates

⸻

Thursday — Community 💚

Community compounds.

Reply to EVERYTHING.

* Emails
* Reviews
* Reddit comments
* Instagram comments
* TikTok comments
* Messages
* Feature requests

Goal:

Make users feel heard.

⸻

Friday — Learn 📚

Spend one focused hour learning.

Possible topics:

* App Store Optimization (ASO)
* Reddit growth
* Instagram growth
* TikTok growth
* Consumer psychology
* Indie app marketing
* Copywriting
* Mobile onboarding
* Product-led growth
* AI content creation

Document one lesson.

⸻

Saturday — Build 🔨

Work only on the highest-value improvements.

Priority order:

1. Bugs
2. User requests
3. UX improvements
4. Small features
5. Technical debt

Avoid large rewrites.

Ship small.

Ship often.

⸻

Sunday — Polish & Ship 🚢

* Test
* Build release
* Submit to App Store
* Write release notes
* Respond to users whose ideas were included
* Update timeline.md
* Update backlog.md

Celebrate every release.

Guiding Principles

* Ship every week.
* Listen more than you talk.
* Build with the community, not just for the community.
* Every feature should solve a real user problem.
* Every week, create at least one new way for someone to discover NorthPaw.
* Consistency beats intensity.
* Trust compounds.
* Small wins become big wins over time.

Long-Term Goal
